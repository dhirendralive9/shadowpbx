What to check

  1. The floating button appears and opens a panel.
  2. "Start call" asks for the microphone (only once per site).
  3. The status goes Connecting → Ringing → Connected, and the timer runs.
  4. The agent's phone rings and audio works both ways.
  5. Mute silences your side; Hang up ends the call on both ends.
  6. In Network → WebRTC the guest appears while the call is up and disappears after it.
  7. The call shows in the CDR with direction web-inbound, with a recording.
